# 30-Day Local Marketing Strategy: Fredericksburg, VA

Goal: more qualified inquiries from people in the Fredericksburg area, using consent-based, inbound methods. No scraping, no contact without permission, no promises about approval, price, financing or vehicle condition. Targets below are goals to measure against, not forecasts.

## Local audit (verified from the repository)
- Name, address (4720 Harrison Rd, Fredericksburg, VA 22408) and phone are consistent in the footer, map, contact page, schema and AutoSEO facts.
- Dealer schema, canonicals, vehicle and article schema and a crawlable article index are in place on the branch (not yet deployed).
- Location pages exist for service areas but come from shared database content and are not in the sitemap. Treat them as thin until each has real, local content. Do not add more.
- There is no Google Business Profile data in the application. Opening hours exist only in site settings and are unverified.
- Blog: 12 live articles, mostly financing topics. There is little local or vehicle-type content.
- Attribution can show which saved leads came from organic search, paid, social, AI or direct.

## Positioning
"My Car" should feel personal: **ownership, belonging, confidence, convenience and a positive buying experience.** The red, yellow and green concept can express moving from a problem to a solution: red is the problem (a car that does not fit your life), yellow is the plan (we help you work it out), green is the solution (the keys in your hand).

Example concepts (copy for review; no claims about approval, price or condition):
1. **"Make it yours."** Real customer key hand-offs from the gallery. Caption: "Another My Car owner. Come see what fits you."
2. **"Red light, yellow light, green light."** A short video in three beats: the stuck feeling, a conversation, the drive away. Ends with a link to schedule a visit.
3. **"Your car, your plan."** A buyer's checklist post and reel (what to check, what to bring to a test drive).
4. **"Neighbors."** Short intros of the team and the lot in Fredericksburg, ending with directions.

## Lead generation, consent-based only
Prioritize: inbound inquiries; local search; opt-in lead forms (Facebook or Instagram lead forms need the privacy-policy link and the right consent); referrals; approved advertising; partnerships. Do not scrape private individuals' emails or phone numbers, buy lists, or message anyone who has not asked to be contacted.

## KPIs (set targets with management; none is assumed)
- Workable leads per day (goal 6 to 10) and leads by source: Daily Leads and Channel Report tabs.
- Appointment requests, and confirmed appointments only where linked.
- Business Profile views, calls and website clicks.
- Search Console clicks and impressions for local terms.
- Reviews received and answered.
- Referral leads and partner leads by tag.
- Share of leads with a known source.

## Vehicle-specific opportunities
Real inventory pages already carry their own schema. Dedicated SUV or truck landing pages need approval and real, unique content. Do not create location doorway pages.

## Approvals needed
Profile access and hours; consent wording and the privacy text; referral reward; any paid budget; the 50-mile and 10-mile area definition.
